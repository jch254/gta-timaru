# Dated street photographs, Canterbury NZ, 1990 to 1996: vehicles visible

Working notes, appended photo by photo. All vehicle identifications are my own judgement from looking at the image, marked confident / probable / guess. Dates and titles are as given by the source catalogue.

Source for the Timaru set: South Canterbury Museum online collection (timdc.pastperfectonline.com), found via DigitalNZ. Local copies in /private/tmp/the03-research/photos/.

## Photo records

### T1. [Don Weir, car] "Coming or going?", Stafford Street, Timaru
- Date (source): 5 October 1991. Black and white press-style photo.
- Page: https://timdc.pastperfectonline.com/photo/F164F8B9-591E-4580-8024-692210153455 (image URL on the same page; local copy photos/tim_42263698.jpg, 950 px wide)

- Vehicles:

  - Behind it, moving: pale boxy 4-door sedan with rectangular lamps, late 1970s; Ford Cortina Mk4/Mk5 or similar. Guess.
  - Left kerb: small 1960s British saloon seen from the rear (Morris 1100/Austin shape, small rear window, dark plate). Guess. Next to it a dark 1980s hatchback/sedan. Guess.
  - Centre, oncoming: dark small 3 or 5-door hatchback, early to mid 1980s Japanese (Ford Laser/Mazda 323/Daihatsu Charade size). Guess.
  - Right kerb, nearest first: two matching 1970s 4-door sedans with chrome bumpers and quad round lamps (Datsun/Toyota/Ford Cortina Mk3 era, cannot split); guess. Then white 1980s sedan with square lamps and a white plate, probable mid-1980s Japanese (Mitsubishi/Toyota). Then a white 3-door hatch signwritten "...aru Herald" on the door (Timaru Herald newspaper car), early 1980s Japanese hatch. Probable for "newspaper pool car", guess for model. Then a row of 1980s sedans, one white station wagon.

### T11. Postcard "Stafford Street, Timaru's shopping centre and main thru fare, South Canterbury"

- The best colour overview of Stafford Street I found: roughly 60 vehicles, both kerbs solidly parked, two moving lanes.
- Vehicles (colour is reliable here, models mostly are not at this size):
  - Foreground left, moving away: mid-blue metallic 4-door sedan, mid-1980s Japanese, wide rectangular tail lamps, plate OH6733 (white plate, black letters). Honda Accord or Mitsubishi Sigma/Galant class. Guess.
  - Foreground centre, oncoming: very dark navy/black small tall hatchback with a Honda "H" grille badge, plate ending "1831". Probable Honda City (early 1980s) or Civic.
  - Foreground right, crossing: bright green 2-door fastback coupe, 1970s Japanese (Toyota Corolla/Sprinter coupe or Datsun coupe), chrome bumpers. Probable for "1970s Japanese coupe", guess for model.
  - Left kerb: red-orange forward-control van with a rear window (Toyota HiAce/Mitsubishi L300 class). Guess. Then a white car, silver sedan, dark red/maroon 1980s sedan (Mitsubishi Sigma class), orange 1970s sedan, pale blue 1980s sedan, white hatch, several more.
  - Centre, oncoming: white forward-control Japanese van (HiAce/LiteAce/L300 class), plain, no signwriting visible. Probable for type.
  - Mid-distance: a red hatchback, a yellow small car, a blue 1980s sedan, a white 4-door.
  - Right kerb: long row of mostly white and pale cars, including at least two white vans or wagons.
- Colour tally I could count with reasonable confidence in the nearer 30 vehicles: white about 10, red/orange/maroon about 6, blue about 5, silver/grey about 4, dark navy/black 2, green 1, yellow 1, brown/beige 1.
- Period details: "Hellaby's" (butcher), "Sport", "Lewis's", "Stafford Mall", "Langwoods", Kodak sign; parallel parking; centre painted flush median with a zebra crossing; overhead Christmas decorations.

### What this suggests for a 1995 street
- The backbone is 5 to 15 year old Japanese cars in the Corolla / Laser / 323 / Sigma / Accord / Bluebird classes, as 4-door sedans and 5-door hatchbacks. That is at least half of anything parked.
- A steady minority (perhaps one car in five by 1995, falling) is 1970s metal: Holden Kingswoods, Valiants, Cortina-size sedans, Corolla and Datsun small cars, with the odd Viva, BMC 1100 or Escort van still earning its keep. The 1996-97 Lincoln Road frames show 20-year-old cars still parked and driving, so these should not be removed from a 1995 scene.
- White is the dominant colour, especially for vans, utes and late-1980s sedans. Then red/maroon, mid and dark blue, silver/grey, beige/gold. 1970s cars carry the loud colours: mustard, orange, bright green, pale blue, yellow.
- Vans are white Japanese forward-control boxes, mostly unsigned; signwriting when present is simple vinyl lettering (a newspaper name on a door, a courier stripe and phone number).
- Utes are plain white Japanese one-tonners with well-side or flat decks; tool loads and canopies are common. No lifted or dressed-up utes seen.
- Wheels: almost everything is on plain steel wheels with hubcaps or plastic covers. I saw alloys on only three or four cars (the white Corolla-class sedan in T32, the maroon big sedan in C6, styled steels on a 1970s coupe in C3).
- Plates: by 1990 nearly all are white with black characters (two letters, four digits); old black plates with silver characters survive only on 1960s-70s cars (T1, T15, T21, T23, T25). A dealer plate surround is visible in C2. I could not resolve registration or warrant stickers in any image.
- Roof racks and tow bars: one clear roof rack (T34 wagon), one tow bar in use (police wagon T7). Too small to see otherwise.
- Rust and wear: sun-faded red/orange paint (T23 Valiant), tired paint on the 1980 Mirage-shape hatch (C3). No heavy rust resolvable at this size.
- Parking: Timaru's Stafford Street had kerbside parallel parking with single and twin-head meters on red-brown posts; five-minute "P5" signs; yellow "Bus Stop Keep Clear" and "Taxi Stand" signs.
- Police: white Holden Commodore (wagon in Timaru, sedan in Christchurch), blue and white chequer side band, "POLICE" in a blue door panel with a red slash, bar light with red and blue lenses, steel wheels.
- Buses: Timaru council fleet in white/cream with blue bands and Lotto roof boards about 1990, a red and white MAN by 1995; Christchurch "Canride" buses mustard-yellow below, white above, in 1991.
- Taxis: I did not find a single clearly identifiable taxi in any viewed image (only a "Taxi Stand" sign in T21). Taxi livery remains an open question for this lane.

### Timaru versus Christchurch
- The comparison is confounded by date: the Timaru street views are mostly about 1990, the Christchurch ones mostly 1996-97. With that caveat:
- Timaru shows more 1970s Australian and British cars per frame, more utes and light trucks parked in the main street, and almost no European cars. The newest cars visible are late 1980s.
- Christchurch shows more late-1980s and 1990s Japanese cars, visible used-car yards on the arterials, a few European cars (Saab, BMW, Lada), courier vans, and much more multi-lane traffic infrastructure (signal mast arms, flush medians, Belisha beacons).
- Common to both: white Japanese vans, white utes, and a tail of 1970s cars still in everyday use.

## Limits of this evidence
1. Dating. Only 9 Timaru images carry a firm day or year in 1990 to 1995 (T1, T3 to T9, T12, T13). The rich Stafford Street set is "circa 1990" with a catalogue range of 1985 to 1995, and at least two frames (T17, T35) look earlier to me. The Christchurch street views are firmly dated but mostly 1996-97, a year or two after the target.
2. No firmly dated 1995 photograph of ordinary kerbside parking was found for either city. The 1995 Timaru images are a parade with the road closed.
3. Resolution. Timaru images are 950 pixels wide with a watermark. Badges are almost never legible, so most model names are class-level judgements. Confident identifications are limited to those with readable lettering (Isuzu, MAN, Saab, Toyota Hilux tailgate, Kawasaki, Honda badge) or unmistakable shapes (Beetle).
4. Sampling bias. Museum photographers were recording buildings and events in the main shopping street on fine days; suburban streets, car parks, rural roads and the highway are missing. Nothing here covers State Highway 1, Ashburton or Temuka.
5. Canterbury Stories images could not be opened directly; I relied on the libraries' Discovery Wall copies, whose dates are sometimes vaguer ("1990s") than the Canterbury Stories record.
6. No taxis, registration stickers or warrant stickers could be confirmed.
