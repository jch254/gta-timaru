- HQ: soft rounded "coke bottle" body, single round headlamps on Belmont/Kingswood, four headlamps on Premier; chrome bumpers.

- HZ: minor exterior update; final Sandman had a four-headlight grille and front spoiler.
- One Tonner: cab chassis truck that kept its own HQ-style front fascia right through HJ, HX and HZ.
- Sandman ute/van: side stripes and a large "Sandman" tailgate logo.

- VR: rounded restyle; "raised tail lamps, recessed high up on the boot lid".

- EF: polycarbonate headlamp lenses; base cars have no grille; Fairmont/Ghia have chrome accents and a (non-functional) grille; XR6/XR8 have twin (quad) headlamps and red or black bumper strips.
